What we collect

BIN 01

We collect information you give us directly, information created by using the app, and a small amount of device data needed to keep orders running smoothly.

How we use it

BIN 02

Your information keeps the marketplace working end to end — matching your vehicle to the right part, routing orders to the correct store, and getting deliveries to the right door.

  • Fulfilling orders — confirming stock with a store, processing payment, arranging delivery or pickup.
  • Compatibility matching — using your saved vehicle details (make, model, year, VIN where provided) to filter parts that actually fit.
  • Support — resolving disputes, refunds, and delivery issues you raise with us.
  • Notifications — order status updates, price drops on saved parts, and account security alerts via push notification or SMS.
  • Safety & fraud prevention — detecting fake accounts, stolen payment methods, and abuse of the store or courier network.
  • Improving the app — understanding which parts and stores are searched for most, in aggregate, to improve search and stock coverage.
We do not use your data to train third-party advertising profiles, and we do not sell your personal information.

Who we share with

BIN 03

AutoHelp Gh is a marketplace, so a limited set of details necessarily passes to the people fulfilling your order.

Registered stores Delivery couriers Payment processor SMS provider Regulators, if required by law
  • Stores receive your order details, contact number, and delivery address to fulfil the order — not your full account history.
  • Delivery partners receive your name, phone number, and address for the specific order they're carrying.
  • Payment processing is handled by Paystack; AutoHelp Gh never sees or stores your full card or Mobile Money PIN.
  • SMS providers (used for order and delivery confirmations) receive your phone number and the message content only.
  • We may disclose information where required by a valid legal request from a Ghanaian court or regulatory authority.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Payments & Mobile Money

BIN 04

All card and Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money) transactions are processed by Paystack, a licensed payment service provider. When you pay through the app:

  • Your card number and MoMo PIN are entered directly into Paystack's secure checkout, not into AutoHelp Gh.
  • We receive a transaction reference and status only — enough to confirm your order was paid for.
  • Refunds are issued back to the original payment method through Paystack.

Location & delivery

BIN 05

We ask for location access to show nearby stores, estimate delivery time, and route your courier.

  • Precise GPS location is only used while placing or tracking an active delivery.
  • You can browse and use store pickup without granting location access — you'll just need to enter your area manually.
  • Saved addresses (home, work, garage) are stored so you don't have to re-enter them each time; you can delete these at any time from Account Settings.

Keeping it secure

BIN 07
  • Data in transit is encrypted with TLS; passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.
  • Access to customer data inside AutoHelp Gh is limited to staff who need it to do their job, with logged access.
  • Payment details never touch our servers — they stay inside Paystack's PCI-DSS compliant systems.
  • If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we'll notify affected users and the Data Protection Commission as required by law.

Your rights

BIN 08

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and as a matter of our own policy, you can:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate account or vehicle information directly in the app.
  • Delete your account and associated personal data, subject to records we're legally required to retain.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data uses, like marketing notifications, at any time in Settings.
  • Object or complain to us directly, or to Ghana's Data Protection Commission, if you believe your data has been mishandled.

To exercise any of these, reach out via the contact details in Bin 11 — we aim to respond within 14 days.

Children

BIN 09

AutoHelp Gh is built for licensed drivers, mechanics, and vehicle owners. The app is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn a minor has created an account, we'll close it and delete the associated data.

Policy changes

BIN 10

As AutoHelp Gh adds features — new payment options, new delivery partners, new regions — this policy will be updated to match. Material changes will be flagged in-app before they take effect, and the "Last reviewed" date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version.
